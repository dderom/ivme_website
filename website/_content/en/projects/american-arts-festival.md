**2009 · Historical project**

Reports from 2009 describe an exchange between musicians from Baylor University and Flemish partners. The programme combined American repertoire and contemporary Flemish music. Cantabile Gent collaborated with IVME on a concert mass within the festival.

This description is based on a historical reconstruction using external sources. It is not a current call or activity.
