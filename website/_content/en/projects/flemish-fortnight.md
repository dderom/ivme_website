**2006 · Historical project**

According to external accounts, IVME organised a Flemish Fortnight with Baylor University in September 2006. Flemish musicians, composers and musicologists introduced audiences in Texas to Flemish music.

This description is based on a historical reconstruction using external sources. It is not a current call or activity.
