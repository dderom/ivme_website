**2013 · Historical project**

The Royal Flemish Academy’s 2013 yearbook names IVME as a partner of PodiJaC: Podium Jongeren Academie Concerten. The series was a collaboration between several organisations; individual concerts are not attributed exclusively to IVME here.

This description is based on a historical reconstruction using external sources. It is not a current call or activity.
